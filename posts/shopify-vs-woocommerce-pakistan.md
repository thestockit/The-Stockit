# Shopify vs WooCommerce for Pakistan: Which Store Platform Is Right for You?

Choosing between **Shopify and WooCommerce** is one of the first big decisions for a Pakistani online store. Both can sell successfully in Pakistan — but they are very different in **cost, payments, hosting and control**.

This guide compares them honestly, in PKR, so you can pick the platform that fits your business. If you want help building either one, our [**e-commerce services hub**](/services/e-commerce) is a good starting point.

---

## Quick comparison at a glance

| | Shopify | WooCommerce |
|---|---|---|
| **Starting cost** | Monthly subscription in USD | Free plugin, but you pay for hosting + domain |
| **Local payments** | JazzCash/Easypaisa via apps | Full control — any gateway |
| **Hosting** | Included (Shopify hosts everything) | You arrange hosting yourself |
| **Design** | Easy themes | Full control, more effort |
| **Control** | Limited by Shopify rules | Complete ownership |
| **Best for** | Fast launches, fashion & D2C brands | Large catalogues, custom flows, dealer networks |

---

## Cost in PKR: what you actually pay

**Shopify** charges a monthly subscription in US dollars. That means your cost moves with the exchange rate — a real consideration for a Pakistani business budgeting in PKR. On top of the plan, Shopify takes a small transaction fee unless you use Shopify Payments, which is not available in Pakistan, so most local stores use third-party payment apps.

**WooCommerce** is free software, but you pay for **hosting, a domain and an SSL certificate**. Good Pakistani-friendly hosting can cost a few thousand rupees a month. You own everything, and there are no transaction fees charged by the platform itself.

The honest summary: WooCommerce is usually cheaper to run at scale, but Shopify is simpler to start and includes hosting. If you already know you want [**Shopify development**](/shopify-development) or [**CMS development**](/cms-development), jump straight to those pages.

## Local payments: JazzCash, Easypaisa and WhatsApp

This is where Pakistan-specific experience matters most.

With **WooCommerce**, you have full control. We can connect **JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer and card payments** directly, plus build custom order flows — for example, a dealer network that orders on WhatsApp. For large catalogues and custom requirements, a [**custom web development**](/web-development) build gives you the same flexibility without platform limits.

With **Shopify**, you are limited to the payment apps available in the Shopify app store for Pakistan. Good local options exist, but you work within Shopify's rules, and some payment setups are easier to achieve on an open platform.

If you are unsure what to integrate, read our guide to the [**best payment gateways for websites in Pakistan**](/blog/best-payment-gateway-for-your-website-in-pakistan).

## Hosting and speed

**Shopify** hosts everything for you — fast, secure, no maintenance. This is genuinely convenient, especially for a small team.

**WooCommerce** runs on your own hosting, so speed and uptime depend on your host and how the site is built. That can be an advantage: a well-optimised WooCommerce store hosted on a good Pakistani or international host can be very fast for local visitors.

---

## Design and control

**Shopify** themes get you a professional store quickly. You can customise a lot, but deep customisation still happens inside Shopify's rules.

**WooCommerce** gives you complete control over design, checkout, shipping and every piece of code. That matters if you need bilingual **Urdu + English** product pages, unusual shipping rules, or a custom checkout flow that matches how your customers actually buy.

---

## Which one should you choose?

**Choose Shopify if:**
- You want to launch fast with minimal technical work.
- You run a fashion, food or D2C brand selling a focused catalogue.
- You are happy paying a monthly fee and letting Shopify handle hosting.

**Choose WooCommerce if:**
- You have a large catalogue, custom integrations or a dealer network.
- You want full control over payments and checkout.
- You prefer PKR-friendly hosting costs over a USD subscription.

Many Pakistani brands start on Shopify to test the market and later move to a custom build as their catalogue grows. Both paths work — the key is matching the platform to your stage. Whichever you choose, pair your store with local [**SEO services**](/seo-services) so shoppers find it on Google.
