## How we reviewed InMotion Hosting

Before the numbers, a word on method. We provisioned a fresh shared-hosting account on the Launch plan in June 2026, installed WordPress, and ran continuous monitoring for 30 days. For TTFB, we used a single test agent hitting the site from three locations — the US East Coast, the US West Coast and the UK — to reflect InMotion's actual data-centre footprint. For uptime, we polled the site every 60 seconds and cross-checked the results against an external monitoring service.

## Performance and infrastructure

The chart above shows average TTFB from our test locations, compared against hosts we reviewed under the same method. InMotion's 420 ms figure is perfectly usable but mid-pack — faster than Bluehost in our tests, slower than Hostinger, A2 Hosting and Scala Hosting.

Three infrastructure choices explain where InMotion sits:

1. **NVMe storage.** Every InMotion shared plan now ships with NVMe SSD drives. NVMe reads translate directly into faster PHP processing and quicker database queries, and it is a genuinely modern storage stack rather than the SATA SSDs still common at this price point.
2. **LiteSpeed web server.** InMotion runs LiteSpeed across its shared platform, with free LiteSpeed Cache support for WordPress. Under concurrency, LiteSpeed is far more efficient than Apache, which keeps queueing delay low when a post goes viral.
3. **Free CDN via QUIC.cloud.** InMotion has integrated QUIC.cloud's CDN into its shared plans at no extra cost. It is a real improvement over running naked origin-only hosting, and it helps claw back some of the latency penalty of having only US data centres — but it is not a substitute for a PoP near your audience.

The honest caveat is geography. InMotion's two data centres are both in the United States. If your visitors are in Pakistan, the UK or Southeast Asia, every request travels across an ocean before the CDN takes over, and you will feel it in TTFB and Core Web Vitals. Hostinger, SiteGround and A2 all offer you a choice of regional data centres; InMotion does not.

<Callout type="info" title="Why TTFB matters">
TTFB is the time between the browser sending a request and the server starting to respond. It is the largest single contributor to Google's Core Web Vitals LCP score on most websites. A 200 ms improvement in TTFB is often worth more than any theme optimisation combined — which is why hosting comparisons spend so much time on it.
</Callout>

A few things are worth understanding about how InMotion prices things, because they are easy to miss:

- The intro price is only available on longer terms (typically 1–3 years). Month-to-month is dramatically more expensive, so budget for the term upfront.
- **Renewals are higher** — around triple the intro rate on the entry plans. This is standard across the industry, but the gap is real, so plan for it.
- **Free domain** for the first year on all shared plans, plus free SSL on every plan.
- **Free migration** from your current host, performed by their team rather than handed to you as a plugin.
- Unmetered bandwidth and, notably for a shared host, a **90-second PHP max execution time** on higher plans — historically InMotion has been friendlier than most hosts to slightly heavier WordPress plugins and cron jobs.

The entry Core plan is genuinely cheap, but it comes with a cap of one website. The Launch plan is the sweet spot for most readers: multiple websites, daily backups, and enough resources for a growing business. Agencies should look at Power or Pro for the extra website count, higher memory limits and priority support.

## The website builder and onboarding

InMotion bundles a free website builder (based on BoldGrid) with its shared plans, and in our experience it is competent rather than exciting. If you want a drag-and-drop editor that produces a decent small-business site without touching code, it works: pick a theme, fill in sections, publish. It handles mobile responsiveness out of the box, and it integrates with the free SSL and email that come with your account.

Where the builder trails the competition is in depth. The block library is smaller than Wix or Squarespace, e-commerce templates are basic, and if you want anything beyond a brochure-style site you will end up installing WordPress and a page builder anyway. For most of our readers, the better path is the one-click WordPress installer (Softaculous, and InMotion also offers a streamlined WordPress wizard on newer plans), then a lightweight theme. Onboarding is quick: from signup to a working WordPress site with SSL active, we were live in roughly ten minutes.

## Customer support: 24/7, US-based

Support is where InMotion justifies its premium positioning. Every plan includes 24/7 support from teams based in the United States, across live chat, phone and ticketing. That US-based, phone-reachable model is genuinely rare in 2026 — Hostinger has no phone line, and Bluehost's phone queues are notoriously variable.

In our testing, we opened a dozen support interactions covering a DNS question, a PHP version issue, and a billing query. Live chat response times averaged around **4–5 minutes**, which is good, and the agents were competent and — notably — empowered to actually change settings on our account rather than escalate everything. The phone line, which we used twice, had hold times under five minutes on both calls, which we would call above average for the industry.

The honest weaknesses: chat agents occasionally read from a script before engaging with the specifics of your problem, and the depth of knowledge varies by agent. Advanced troubleshooting (server-level tweaks, exotic PHP settings) sometimes requires a ticket, and ticket responses run 2–6 hours — fine for non-urgent work, not fine if your site is down. For day-to-day questions, though, InMotion's support is clearly in the top tier of shared hosts.

<Callout type="warning" title="When support will be tested">
InMotion's support is excellent for hosting questions — migrations, email setup, SSL, PHP settings. If your application code itself is broken, no host's support desk can save you, and InMotion is no exception. For genuinely mission-critical sites, pair the host with an independent monitoring service rather than relying on support to notice an outage first.
</Callout>

## Security features

InMotion ships a credible security stack on every shared plan rather than selling it as an add-on:

- **Free SSL certificates** on every plan, auto-renewed, with Let's Encrypt as the default.
- **Automatic backups** — weekly on Core, daily on Launch and above, restorable from the control panel in a few clicks.
- **Imunify360** on Power and Pro, giving you a real-time web application firewall and malware scanning. On Core and Launch, you will want a reputable security plugin for malware scanning, since the WAF is not included.
- **Free website security** included via the account dashboard, covering basic hardening and malware removal support on affected accounts.

We ran a standard vulnerability scan against a fresh install and found the baseline hardening — secure headers, disabled directory listing, protected `wp-config` — already in place. InMotion is also one of the few shared hosts we have tested that ships brute-force protection enabled on the control panel login by default. None of this stops a determined attacker targeting your own application code, but the platform-level hygiene is materially better than the bare minimum you see on some budget hosts.

## The 90-day money-back guarantee

This is InMotion's signature move, and it deserves attention. Most hosts give you 30 days; InMotion gives you **90 days** to decide, with a full refund on shared hosting if you cancel within the window. It is the longest money-back guarantee in the mainstream hosting industry, full stop.

Why does this matter beyond the obvious? Two reasons. First, hosting problems rarely surface in the first week — the slow database query, the support ticket that goes unanswered, the 3am outage — and 90 days gives you real time to evaluate a platform as your business uses it. Second, it de-risks the long-term commitment that InMotion's pricing model needs: to get the good intro rate you typically sign up for a year or more, and the 90-day window is the safety net that makes that commitment reasonable.

A few honest caveats. The guarantee applies to shared and reseller hosting; VPS and dedicated plans have shorter windows (we have seen 30 days quoted for these). Refunds are processed back to your original payment method, and domain registration fees are typically deducted if you used the free domain. And a free site migration is a service — if you cancel, moving your site back out is on you.

## Who is InMotion Hosting best for?

InMotion is the right host if you run — or plan to run — a US-facing small business or service site, and you value being able to pick up a phone and talk to a competent human in the middle of the night. It is also an excellent choice if you are migrating an existing site and want someone else to do the heavy lifting, or if you simply want the longest possible trial period while you decide.

It is the wrong choice if your audience is primarily in Pakistan, Europe or Asia, where the two US-only data centres put you at a latency disadvantage that a CDN can only partially fix. It is also the wrong choice if raw price-per-performance is your only metric — Hostinger or Scala will give you faster TTFB for less money, though you give up the phone support and the 90-day guarantee.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is InMotion good for WordPress?

Yes — InMotion is one of the more WordPress-friendly hosts you will find. LiteSpeed hosting with free LiteSpeed Cache, one-click WordPress installation, staging tools on higher plans, and a US-based support team that actually knows WordPress make it a solid home for WP business sites. It lacks SiteGround's polish and Hostinger's raw speed, but it is dependable.

### Does InMotion offer free migration?

Yes. Every InMotion shared plan includes free website migration, and — unusually — it is done by InMotion's own team rather than handed to you as a plugin. They move your files, databases and email, usually within 24 hours. It is one of the strongest free-migration offerings in the industry.

### How fast is InMotion hosting?

In our 30-day tests, median TTFB landed around 420 ms — respectable but mid-pack. NVMe storage, LiteSpeed and a free QUIC.cloud CDN keep it competitive, and US-based visitors will see solid performance. But the two US-only data centres mean visitors in Pakistan, Europe or Asia will notice higher latency than they would get from a host with regional servers.

### Does InMotion host in Pakistan?

No. InMotion's data centres are both in the United States (Los Angeles and Ashburn, Virginia). There is no data centre in Pakistan or anywhere else in Asia. Pakistani visitors will rely on the free CDN to bridge the distance, which helps but does not fully offset the geography.

### What is the InMotion money-back guarantee?

90 days on shared and reseller hosting — the longest in the mainstream industry. VPS and dedicated plans carry shorter windows (typically 30 days). Refunds go back to your original payment method, and the free domain fee is usually deducted if you cancel.
