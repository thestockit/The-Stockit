## How Does a CPA Network Work?

CPA stands for **Cost Per Action**. Affiliates (publishers) are paid a commission each time they drive a user who completes a specific action like a sign-up, download, or purchase. The network (Zeydoo) acts as the middleman, connecting advertisers with affiliates, and handling tracking, payments, and support.

Understanding the model matters because it explains where the money comes from. An advertiser pays Zeydoo to deliver conversions; Zeydoo keeps a margin and passes the rest to you. That means your payout is directly tied to the advertiser's cost-per-acquisition targets, which is why rates vary so much by geo and vertical — a US finance lead is worth more to an advertiser than a Tier-3 email submit, and your commission reflects that.

CPA networks also differ from display ad networks in a crucial way: you are paid for results, not impressions. If you send 10,000 visits and nobody converts, you earn nothing. That is why the network's anti-fraud system, conversion tracking, and offer-to-traffic matching matter just as much as the payout rates themselves.

## Payout Models and Earning Potential

Zeydoo operates primarily on CPA and CPL, but the offer catalog covers the full spectrum of performance models:

- **CPS (Cost Per Sale):** a share of each sale, where available, typically on higher-ticket offers.
