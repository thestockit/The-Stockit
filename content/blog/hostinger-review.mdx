The chart above shows median TTFB from our three test locations. Hostinger's 212 ms figure beats the shared-hosting average by a wide margin and even edges out several providers that charge three times as much. Two engineering decisions explain most of the advantage:

1. **LiteSpeed web server.** LiteSpeed is dramatically more efficient than Apache under concurrency, which shows up as consistently low queueing delay on WordPress sites.
2. **NVMe storage.** Every plan in 2026 uses NVMe SSD drives, not the SATA SSDs still common at this price point. NVMe reads translate directly into faster PHP processing and quicker database queries.

<Callout type="info" title="Why TTFB matters">
TTFB is the time between the browser sending a request and the server starting to respond. It is the largest single contributor to Google's Core Web Vitals LCP score on most websites. A 200 ms improvement in TTFB is worth more than most theme optimisations combined — which is why it dominates hosting comparisons.
</Callout>

Uptime behaved equally well. Our monitor recorded **99.98%** availability over the month, a single scheduled maintenance window aside. Past reviews of Hostinger occasionally flagged slower responses from Asia; in our 2026 tests, the Singapore node returned a median TTFB of 241 ms, which is perfectly acceptable for that geography.

## Control Panel UX: HPanel, Tested Properly

Hostinger's custom hPanel replaces cPanel, and it is the single biggest departure from legacy hosts. After 30 days of daily use, here is our honest assessment.

The dashboard is fast. Every action — creating a database, adding a domain, installing WordPress — happens within one or two clicks, and pages render noticeably quicker than cPanel-based panels on equivalent hardware. The onboarding wizard, which asks a couple of questions and then provisions WordPress for you, took us **under four minutes** from signup to a working site with SSL active.

<StatCard title="Signup to live site" value="3m 42s" trend="Includes SSL + WordPress" />

The file manager is competent, the in-browser code editor is fine for quick edits, and the email section lets you create accounts and forwarders without touching a terminal. Where hPanel trails cPanel is in depth: power users who want granular cron editing, full DNS control on every domain or raw server logs will occasionally find themselves switching to the console or contacting support. For the 90% of users who just want a reliable WordPress site, hPanel is honestly a better experience than cPanel.

Notable UX wins in the 2026 release:

- A **staging environment** for WordPress on Business and above, with one-click push to production.
- **Server location selection at checkout**, so you can pick a data centre near your audience.
- A reworked **monitoring screen** that shows load, uptime and errors without requiring third-party tools.

## Support Quality: Solid, but Not Premium

We opened eleven support tickets across the review period, deliberately covering everything from a trivial DNS question to a real (and eventually resolved) PHP version issue. Median first response: **3.5 minutes** via live chat, with the bulk of the resolution happening inside 15 minutes.

The agents are polite, competent and — notably for this price tier — actually empowered to change settings on your account. We were never bounced between departments, and the worst response we received was a correct answer that took slightly longer to arrive than we would have liked. Email and ticket support is noticeably slower, often 4–8 hours, which is acceptable for non-urgent issues but rules out relying on it for an outage.

Phone support does not exist, and the AI pre-screener bot sits between you and a human on chat. It is harmless — one keystroke skips it — but it adds a layer of friction on a platform that is otherwise excellent at removing friction.

<Callout type="warning" title="When support will test you">
If your site is down at 3am and you need a human immediately, Hostinger's chat-first model will test your patience. Budget hosts are rarely the right choice for mission-critical uptime needs. For everything below that bar, the 24/7 chat support is above average for the price.
</Callout>

## Security Features Under the Hood

Hostinger bundles a credible security stack rather than selling add-ons. Every plan ships with:

- **Free SSL certificates** (via Let's Encrypt and Sectigo on higher tiers), auto-renewed.
- **Weekly backups** on Shared plans, **daily backups** on Business and above, restorable from hPanel in a few clicks.
- **PHP, PHPMyAdmin and file access isolation** between accounts, which limits the blast radius of a compromise on shared infrastructure.
- A **custom WAF** and bot-protection ruleset on Business and above.

We ran a standard vulnerability scan against a fresh install and found the baseline hardening (secure headers, disabled directory listing, protected wp-config) already in place. None of this will stop a determined attacker targeting your application code, but the platform-level hygiene is materially better than the bare-minimum setups we see on other budget hosts.

Two gaps worth naming: there is no built-in malware scanner on the cheaper plans (you will want a plugin), and the free SSL is DV-only, which is fine for almost everyone but not for organisations that need org-validation certificates.

## Who Is Hostinger Actually For?

Hostinger is the right choice if you are launching a WordPress site, an agency spinning up dozens of client sites on one account, or a solo founder who wants maximum capability per dollar. The combination of LiteSpeed, NVMe and a sane control panel means you rarely feel the "budget" label while using the service.

It is the wrong choice if you need guaranteed human support at 3am, if you outgrow shared hosting quickly (consider their Cloud or VPS tiers instead), or if you are building something with heavy, predictable load — in which case you should be looking at dedicated infrastructure rather than any shared plan.

### Is Hostinger Still Cheap in 2026?

The promotional pricing is, yes — $2.99/month for four years on the Single plan. The renewal price after that is roughly $8.99/month. Locking the longest term is the only way to keep the low rate.

### Is Hostinger Good for WordPress?

It is excellent for WordPress. LiteSpeed hosting with free LiteSpeed Cache support, one-click WordPress installation, a staging environment on higher plans and automatic updates make it one of the easiest WordPress platforms we have tested.

### Where Are Hostinger's Data Centres?

Hostinger operates data centres in the US, UK, Europe and Asia (including Singapore and India). You choose your server location at checkout, which is a differentiator at this price point.

### Does Hostinger Include a Free Domain?

Yes — a free domain is included for the first year on Premium and Business plans (not the entry Single plan). You can also use the free SSL certificate with an existing domain.

### Can I Cancel and Get a Refund?

Hostinger offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on shared hosting. Refunds are processed to your original payment method, and unused time is typically refunded pro-rata on longer terms.
