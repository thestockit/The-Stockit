## What is Propeller Ads Network?

Founded in **2011**, Propeller Ads is a **self-serve ad platform** offering **CPM, CPA, and CPC models**. With **8 billion daily ad impressions** and **1B+ monthly audience reach**, it’s one of the largest traffic sources for publishers and advertisers worldwide. The platform grew out of the performance-marketing world, which helps explain its flexibility: instead of forcing every publisher into a single auction model, it lets you pick the monetization style that fits your traffic.

For publishers, the most important thing to understand is that Propeller Ads is not trying to replace AdSense so much as complement it. Many site owners run both networks side by side — using AdSense for display placements and Propeller Ads for formats AdSense does not offer, such as popunders and push notifications. Because the two systems do not compete for the same ad slots in most cases, earnings can genuinely stack.

## Business Model

Propeller Ads provides a **transparent bidding system** with multiple monetization models:

- **CPA (Cost Per Action)**
- **CPL (Cost Per Lead)**
- **CPM (Cost Per Mille)**
- **CPC (Cost Per Click)** on selected formats

Unlike AdSense, which is **CPC-based**, Propeller Ads focuses on **CPM earnings**. This means publishers earn money **for every 1,000 ad impressions**, even if users don’t click. That distinction matters more than most publishers realize. On a CPC network, an empty-clicking visitor earns you nothing; on a CPM network, every pageview still contributes to your balance. For sites with high traffic but average click-through rates — typical of news, tools, and gaming content — CPM can be the better match.

👉 This makes Propeller Ads an excellent option for **new publishers** with growing traffic, who often struggle to hit AdSense’s click volume during the early months.

There is also a managed side to the business. Larger publishers can apply for a dedicated account manager, better rates, and access to premium ad demand. For everyone else, the self-serve dashboard handles targeting, placements, and payouts without human intervention.

## Ad Formats Offered

Propeller Ads offers a variety of ad formats to maximize revenue. This breadth is one of its biggest strengths — you are not locked into a single placement type, and you can test which format your audience tolerates best.

### OnClick Ads (Popunders)

The format Propeller Ads is best known for. A popunder opens a new browser tab silently behind the user’s current window, so it is less likely to be blocked by default browser pop-up blockers. It is also the format with the highest typical RPM. The catch is user experience: if overused, popunders frustrate visitors and can hurt your engagement metrics. Run them on pages with low bounce sensitivity and test frequency caps.

### In-Page Push Ads

These render inside the page as a notification-style unit — typically a small icon with an accompanying banner that appears on a corner of the screen. They do not require the user to subscribe to push notifications, which makes them easy to implement. Because they are non-intrusive and available on mobile and desktop alike, in-page push is a good middle-ground format for sites that want revenue without popunder aggression.

### Push Notifications

Web push requires visitors to opt in. Once they do, you can serve ads to their notification tray even when they are not on your site. The opt-in requirement lowers fill, but the audience that subscribes tends to receive a steady stream of impressions, which can add a predictable base of revenue. Push works especially well for news, deals, and content with a returning readership.

### Interstitial Ads

Full-screen ads that appear between page transitions — typically when a visitor clicks from one article to the next. They command full attention and deliver strong viewability, but they interrupt the reading flow. Publishers usually enable them on high-traffic pages and disable them on the pages where they want to keep users engaged, such as the landing page.

### Native Ads

Native units match the look and feel of your site rather than screaming "ad." They tend to earn lower RPM than popunders but offer the least disruptive experience, which can protect dwell time and repeat visits. For publishers focused on user experience, native is often the format that runs always-on.

### Banner Ads

Propeller Ads supports **banner ads (all standard sizes)** — leaderboards, skyscrapers, rectangles, and mobile-responsive units. These slot into AdSense-style placements and are the easiest way to get started since the code is a simple snippet. Their RPM depends heavily on your traffic geography, but they are a reliable baseline.

These formats give publishers flexibility to choose ads that balance **user experience and revenue generation**.

## Requirements to Join

The good news? **No minimum traffic requirements.** 🎉  
However:

1. Websites must not host illegal or harmful content (adult, extremist, gambling, malware, etc.).
2. Sites under construction or incomplete are not accepted.
3. While beginners can join, **decent traffic improves earnings.**
4. The site should have real, human visitors — incentivized or bot traffic can lead to account review.

Approval is generally quick for legitimate sites. The moderation team checks content and site structure rather than traffic volume, which means a brand-new blog can be monetized in its first week. That is a sharp contrast to networks that demand minimum pageviews before considering your application.

## Who Should Use Propeller Ads?

The network is a good fit if you are a new blogger, run a tool or gaming site with high traffic but low click-through, monetize non-US audiences and want predictable CPM income, or already use AdSense and want to add push, popunder, or interstitial formats on top.

It is a worse fit if your priority is premium brand advertisers, if you refuse to run popunders under any circumstances, or if you monetize a tiny niche with almost no traffic — in that case the earnings will be modest regardless of which network you choose.

## FAQs

### How much can I earn with Propeller Ads?

Earnings depend on traffic volume, geography, and format mix. With Tier 1 traffic and popunders running, typical RPM can range from a few dollars to well over $30, while banners on Tier 3 traffic may earn under $1 per 1,000 impressions. The figures above are illustrative, not guarantees.

### Does Propeller Ads work with AdSense on the same site?

Yes. Most publishers run both, using AdSense for display placements and Propeller Ads for popunder, push, and interstitial formats. Keep the total number of ad units reasonable so your content remains readable.

### Is there a minimum traffic requirement to join?

No. Propeller Ads has no minimum traffic requirement, though sites must be fully built out, avoid prohibited content, and attract real visitors. Higher traffic simply earns more because earnings scale with impressions.

### How long does payout take?

Payouts are processed on a monthly cycle once you cross the threshold. The $5 minimum applies to PayPal and WebMoney, while wire transfers require $500. Processing usually adds a few days after the cycle closes.

### Are popunders really safe to use?

They are safe in the sense that the ads come from a moderated network, but they are aggressive by design. Limit them to pages with low bounce sensitivity, use frequency caps, and test. If your audience complains, switch to in-page push or native formats.
