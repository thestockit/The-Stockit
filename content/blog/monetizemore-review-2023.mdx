## What Is MonetizeMore?

MonetizeMore is a **Canada-based ad revenue optimization company** founded in 2010. Unlike a simple ad network that just serves ads, MonetizeMore acts as an **outsourced SSP (Supply Side Platform)** — it manages the entire technical side of selling your ad inventory, from header bidding to floor prices, and optimizes it continuously.

The company was one of the first outsourced SSPs to focus on **ad optimization rather than just ad management**. Instead of setting up your stack and leaving you to it, MonetizeMore treats revenue optimization as an ongoing, technology-driven process. That philosophy is baked into everything it offers.

A key milestone came in **2014**, when MonetizeMore partnered with Google and gained access to **AdX, AdSense, and DEP (Dynamic Allocation)**. This partnership is the backbone of the product. It is what allows MonetizeMore to put your inventory in front of Google's premium programmatic demand, which is normally reserved for sites that meet Google's strict AdX requirements.

In short: MonetizeMore sits between you and the world's biggest ad buyers. It handles the technical integration, the auction mechanics, and the optimization, and you get better RPMs without having to manage a complex ad stack yourself.

## How MonetizeMore Makes Money for Publishers

MonetizeMore operates on a **managed service model with a revenue share**. There are no monthly platform fees for the standard setup — the company earns its cut as a percentage of your ad revenue, which aligns its incentives with yours. If your RPMs don't improve, MonetizeMore doesn't get paid either. This pay-for-performance structure is one of the main reasons publishers trust it.

The typical arrangement works like this:

- You hand over ad delivery to MonetizeMore's team, which manages your Google AdX account and header bidding wrapper.
- The platform sells your inventory across multiple demand sources simultaneously, so buyers compete for each impression.
- MonetizeMore takes a revenue share of the resulting earnings, and you receive the balance on a set payment schedule.

This is the same **rev-share model** popularized by managed platforms like **AdThrive** and **Mediavine**, where the company earns more when you earn more. Unlike those networks, which are known primarily as AdSense alternatives for bloggers, MonetizeMore positions itself as a higher-tier, ad-tech-led option for publishers who want direct AdX access and aggressive optimization.

In practice, the biggest gains usually go to publishers moving from AdSense-only or network-only monetization to a properly managed header bidding stack with AdX in the mix. A site that is already well-optimized with multiple demand partners may see a far more modest improvement.

## Best Features

MonetizeMore offers a set of features that genuinely set it apart from ordinary ad networks. Here is what you actually get:

- **Google Certified Partner status** — a signal of vetting and ongoing compliance with Google's standards.
- **AI-powered header bidding** — the platform's core technology, which routes each impression through multiple demand sources and uses machine learning to predict which combinations of buyers, floors, and formats will earn the most.
- **Full programmatic advertising solutions** — prebid integration, ad server management, and demand partner management are all handled for you.
- **Direct access to Google AdX** — the premium inventory marketplace that most publishers cannot access on their own.
- **Fast-loading ads** — MonetizeMore optimizes ad delivery to minimize the impact on page speed, which protects your Core Web Vitals and search rankings.
- **No DNS takeover** — many managed platforms (most famously Ezoic) require you to point your domain's DNS at their servers. MonetizeMore does not, which means your site keeps its existing DNS, SSL, and caching setup untouched. For publishers who have tried DNS-based systems before, this is a meaningful difference.
- **No contract lock-ins** — you are not trapped in a long-term agreement. If the platform underperforms, you can leave.
- **Free setup** — DFP (Google Ad Manager) and header bidding configuration are handled by their team at no charge.

The combination of direct AdX access, AI optimization, and no DNS takeover is what separates MonetizeMore from the crowd. Most networks give you one of those; few give you all three.

## How the Setup Process Works

The onboarding process is more involved than pasting a single script, but MonetizeMore's team does most of the heavy lifting:

1. **Application and review.** You apply with your site URL and traffic figures. The team checks your traffic volume, content quality, and policy compliance before approving you.
2. **AdX application.** MonetizeMore manages the Google AdX approval process on your behalf. This is a major advantage — qualifying for AdX directly requires substantial traffic and often repeated applications.
3. **Ad stack integration.** Their engineers integrate your site with Google Ad Manager and set up the header bidding wrapper, demand partners, and ad units.
4. **Optimization.** Once live, the AI continuously tests floor prices, demand sources, and ad placements. The human team also monitors performance and adjusts manually where needed.

Because there is no DNS takeover, the migration is relatively painless. You keep your existing infrastructure and simply swap the ad-serving layer. The free setup also removes the cost barrier that pushes some publishers toward DIY stacks.

## Publisher Requirements

MonetizeMore is not for everyone, and its requirements are the clearest demonstration of that:

- **Minimum of 500,000 monthly pageviews.** This is the headline number and it is high. For comparison, many premium managed networks start considering sites at 50,000 monthly sessions, and AdThrive's typical bar is around 100,000. At half a million, MonetizeMore is filtering for publishers with genuinely substantial, established traffic.
- **100% compliance with Google AdX policies.** Your content must be fully compliant with Google's strictest policy standards. Anything borderline — low-quality content, copyright issues, or policy-gray monetization — will be rejected.
- **No language restrictions.** The platform accepts sites in multiple languages, which makes it more globally accessible than some competitors that focus on English-language inventory.

If you are a small or growing blog, these requirements will likely disqualify you for now. That is not a criticism of the product, but it is a critical practical fact to know before you invest time applying.

## Pricing and Revenue Share Model

There is no upfront cost to join MonetizeMore, and no fixed monthly SaaS fee for the managed setup. Instead, the company earns a **revenue share** on your earnings. Because MonetizeMore is not publicly transparent about its exact percentage in the way some networks publish a clean 70/30 or 80/20 split, it is worth asking for the current rate during your application conversation.

The honest way to evaluate the pricing is to compare it against what you would pay in effort. A DIY header bidding stack means managing prebid.js yourself, negotiating with demand partners, maintaining floor prices, and staying on top of Google policy changes. For a publisher with half a million pageviews, that effort is real money. MonetizeMore's cut is effectively the price of outsourcing all of it — plus the benefit of its team's expertise and its AdX access, which many publishers could not obtain on their own at any price.

## Who Should Use MonetizeMore (and Who Shouldn't)

MonetizeMore is the right fit if you:

- Have **established traffic above roughly 500,000 monthly pageviews**.
- Are currently under-monetized — for example, running AdSense only or a single ad network without header bidding.
- Want **Google AdX access** without fighting the application process yourself.
- Prefer to **outsource ad management** rather than maintain a DIY prebid stack.
- Value a **pay-for-performance model** with no contract lock-in.

It is the wrong fit if you:

- Are a **new or small publisher** below the traffic threshold — you will not qualify, and you would be better served by AdSense, Mediavine, or Ezoic while you grow.
- Have a **niche with very low-value traffic** where even premium demand will not move RPMs dramatically.
- Want **full manual control** over every bidder, floor, and placement rather than handing that over to an optimization team.
- Need **instant, fully automated reporting** with no human follow-up.

The clearest way to think about it: MonetizeMore is a **growth and scale tool**, not a starting point. It is built for publishers who have already proven their traffic and now want to squeeze maximum revenue from it.

## Honest Limitations

No review is complete without acknowledging what MonetizeMore does not do well:

- **The traffic bar is genuinely high.** Half a million monthly pageviews is a real filter, and it means the platform is simply unavailable to most sites. If you are on the cusp of the threshold, it may be worth growing first and applying later.
- **Reporting automation is incomplete.** While the core revenue data is visible, some publishers report that parts of the reporting require manual interpretation or support-team involvement. If you want every metric fully automated and self-serve, this is a gap.
- **The revenue share percentage is not transparent up front.** You will likely need to discuss it during onboarding, which makes apples-to-apples comparisons with AdThrive or Mediavine harder.
- **Results are not guaranteed.** The famous 50–400% uplifts come from self-reported publisher cases and are not a promise. A site already running a well-tuned multi-demand stack may see only incremental gains.

None of these are disqualifying — they are the normal trade-offs of a managed premium service. But they deserve to be stated plainly rather than buried in marketing.

Yes. MonetizeMore has operated since 2010, is a Google Certified Publishing Partner, and has won Google Innovation Awards in 2021 and 2022. It reports managing over 1,000 websites, serving more than 15 billion monthly impressions, and paying out over $100 million to publishers.

MonetizeMore is best used as your primary managed ad stack, since it replaces your existing header bidding and ad server configuration. Because there is no long-term contract, you can leave if it underperforms, but running it side-by-side with another managed platform is not the intended setup.
