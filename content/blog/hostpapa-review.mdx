Fine for most small sites; worth knowing if you are comparing it against managed WordPress offerings.

## Security and backups

Security at HostPapa starts with the basics done properly: free SSL certificates on every plan, secure data centres, and account isolation on shared servers. Malware scanning and site-lock protection exist, but you should know they are typically sold as add-ons on lower plans rather than bundled in — read the checkout screen carefully, because the add-on pricing is one of the places your bill can creep up.

Backups are the honest weak point on entry tiers. Business Pro includes automated backups; on Starter and Business you will want to either enable the paid backup add-on or rely on your own plugin-based backups. Our rule of thumb stands: never depend on any host's backups alone. Configure your own off-site backup for anything you cannot afford to lose.

The overall security posture is adequate and industry-standard, and the support team is capable of walking you through security fixes — which, for a beginner, is arguably the most valuable security feature there is.

It is a small business selling to small businesses, and it leans into that alignment with community and environmental messaging that has been consistent for years.

We should be clear about what this does and does not mean. It does not mean the servers themselves run off solar panels on the roof; it means HostPapa offsets its electricity consumption through verified renewable energy credits. That is a real, verifiable commitment and more than most hosts can say. If running a greener business is a priority for your brand, this is one of the easiest hosting decisions you will ever make.
