## What Is InterServer?

InterServer is an independently owned hosting company founded in 1999, operating its own data centers in Secaucus, New Jersey. Twenty-plus years in the hosting business is a meaningful signal by itself: most hosts that were household names in 1999 are either gone, resold, or folded into giant conglomerates. InterServer has stayed independent, which shows up in how it behaves — no aggressive upsell culture, no marketing blitzes, and a reputation for plain-spoken service.

The company's positioning is deliberately unglamorous. While bigger names like GoDaddy and Hostinger spend heavily on advertising, InterServer reinvests in infrastructure and keeps its pricing straightforward. Its philosophy is simple: sell good hosting at a fair price, and let the product do the talking. For a new publisher who just wants a site that works, that understated approach is refreshing.

## The Price Lock Guarantee

The single most distinctive thing about InterServer — and the reason it keeps appearing in "best cheap hosting" conversations — is the Price Lock Guarantee. Here is how it works in plain terms: the price you pay when you sign up is the price you keep paying, for as long as your account stays active. InterServer does not raise your rate when your term renews.

That is rare. Nearly every mainstream budget host — Hostinger, HostGator, Namecheap, and countless others — advertises a rock-bottom promotional price for your first term, then quietly more than doubles the rate at renewal. The promo price is a loss leader; the renewal is where the margin comes back. InterServer simply does not play that game.

The original testing that inspired this review noted a concrete example: the Standard plan launched at $5/mo, and later moved up to $7/mo for new signups. Customers who signed up at $5 kept paying $5. That kind of grandfathering is almost unheard of in shared hosting, and it is the honest reason the renewal-price anxiety that plagues other hosts simply does not apply here.

## InterServer Plans and Pricing

InterServer keeps its shared lineup deliberately simple: three plans built on identical core features, with the differences in allocated resources rather than functionality. That is a smart design choice for beginners, because the plan you pick never changes the feature set — you only scale resources as your site grows.

- **Standard Plan** → typical ~$7/mo (formerly ~$5/mo)
- **Boost 2 Plan** → typical ~$10/mo
- **Boost 4 Plan** → typical ~$20/mo

All three share the same underlying features: unlimited storage and bandwidth on the Standard plan, free SSL certificates, free website migration, and access to the same server infrastructure. The Boost tiers simply allocate more CPU power, more RAM, and more concurrent connections for sites with heavier traffic. For a small or medium website — a typical blog, a portfolio, a local business site — the Standard plan is more than enough, and the price lock makes it even easier to justify.

One detail worth calling out: because InterServer runs its own data centers and uses the same infrastructure across every plan, even the entry-level Standard plan benefits from the same hardware as the top tier. You are not buying into a stripped-down "budget server" the way you do with some hosts' cheapest tiers.

For a new publisher, the combination that matters most is cPanel familiarity, free SSL, and free migration. Moving a first site is often the scariest technical step, and InterServer removing the cost and much of the friction is a genuinely useful convenience.

## Performance Expectations

Performance is where InterServer earns much of its reputation. The original testing that inspired this review found a media-heavy website loaded in about **1.3 seconds** on the $5 Standard plan — comfortably faster than Namecheap and Hostinger under similar conditions, which took roughly three times as long in the same tests. Treat that number as typical rather than a guarantee; load times always depend on your theme, images, plugins, and reader location.

The architectural reason InterServer performs well on budget plans is simple: it runs the same infrastructure across all plans, so entry-level users are not quarantined onto slow, oversold servers. InterServer also integrates Cloudflare at the server level, which pairs with its caching to keep time-to-first-byte competitive for a shared host.

## Security: InterShield Included

Security is the area where budget hosts usually nickel-and-dime you, and it is the area where InterServer quietly differentiates itself. Every plan ships with **InterShield**, InterServer's proprietary security suite, at no extra cost. Its components include:

- Firewall protection at the server level
- Automatic virus scanning
- Malicious file detection
- Web attack defense (including DDoS mitigation)
- Server-side caching with Cloudflare integration

For a new publisher, the practical upside is simple: you get a genuinely solid security baseline without paying for add-ons like SiteLock or CodeGuard that many hosts push at checkout. Combined with the free SSL certificates, a new InterServer account starts reasonably hardened out of the box, and the server-side caching doubles as a performance feature.

## Support and Getting Help

InterServer's support is available 24/7 through live chat and email, and in our experience the agents are genuinely technical — they can edit server-level settings rather than reading from a script. Typical response times are reasonable, and because InterServer runs its own infrastructure, support can act on issues directly rather than filing tickets with an upstream provider.

Two honest caveats. First, there is no phone support — if you prefer calling a human, InterServer will not be the host for you. Second, the knowledge base and documentation are functional rather than polished; because the control panel is standard cPanel, most answers live in the wider cPanel ecosystem anyway. For the overwhelming majority of shared-hosting questions a new publisher will have, the live chat handles it fine.

## Student Hosting Deal

InterServer offers one of the friendliest student perks in the industry: **one full year of free hosting** when you sign up with a `.edu` email address. Even without a `.edu` address, you can contact support and request a student plan — approvals are not guaranteed, but it is a documented path.

This makes InterServer an unusually good fit for students launching a portfolio, a class project, a club website, or a personal blog. A year of real hosting with a real domain, SSL, and cPanel is far more useful for learning than a free subdomain on a site builder — and if the project grows, the account converts to a normal plan with the price lock intact.

## ASP.net (Windows) Hosting

Most shared hosting reviews never mention this, because most shared hosts are Linux-only. InterServer is one of the few affordable providers that also offers **ASP.net / Windows hosting** — a genuinely rare feature at budget prices.

This matters if you need to run:

- Microsoft SQL Server databases
- Microsoft Access databases
- Windows-specific applications and .NET code

For a developer or small team building on the Microsoft stack, InterServer can be a surprising one-stop shop: Linux plans for the front-end and a Windows plan for the application, both under one account and both at budget prices. It is a niche advantage, but it is a real one, and it explains why InterServer keeps a loyal following among Windows developers specifically.

## Uptime, Refunds, and Compensation

InterServer backs its service with a **99.9% uptime guarantee**, and the compensation mechanism is genuinely user-friendly: if your site's uptime falls below the guarantee, you track the outage, submit a support ticket, and InterServer credits your account based on the severity and duration of the downtime.

On top of that, new accounts are covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee on shared plans, so your first month is effectively risk-free. The compensation-for-outages model is the kind of detail that rarely shows up in marketing, and it reflects the same no-nonsense approach as the price lock.

## Limitations: What InterServer Does Not Do

No review is honest without the flip side. Here is where InterServer falls short, and why it is not for everyone:

- **No phone support** — live chat and tickets only
- **A dated-feeling control panel and branding** — InterServer prioritizes function over polish
- **US-centric infrastructure** — data centers are in the US, so readers in Europe, Asia, or Australia may see higher latencies
- **Fewer beginner hand-holding features** than managed WordPress hosts — you get solid shared hosting, not a guided setup wizard
- **Boost tiers still run on shared infrastructure**, so they are not a substitute for a real VPS on high-traffic sites

None of these are deal-breakers for the typical new publisher. But if you are launching a site primarily for an audience outside North America, or you want white-glove onboarding, a managed WordPress host or one with data centers closer to your readers will serve you better.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is InterServer cheap after the first term?

Yes — that is the entire point of the Price Lock Guarantee. The rate you sign up at is the rate you keep paying on renewal, as long as your account stays active. Where most hosts double or triple the price after year one, InterServer does not raise your rate at all. New pricing applies only to new customers.

### What does the InterServer Standard plan include?

The Standard plan (typical ~$7/mo) includes unlimited storage, unlimited bandwidth, unlimited websites, free SSL certificates, free weekly backups, free site migration, a cPanel control panel, and the InterShield security suite — all at the locked-in price. It is enough for small and medium sites, with Boost tiers available if you need more CPU and RAM.

### Does InterServer offer free migration?

Yes. InterServer's team will migrate your existing website for free when you switch from another host, which removes one of the biggest friction points in moving a live site. The migration covers standard cPanel-based transfers; complex or custom setups may require a support ticket.

### Does InterServer offer student discounts?

Yes. InterServer provides one year of free hosting when you sign up with a `.edu` email address, and students without one can contact support to request a student plan. It is a genuine, long-standing perk, not a marketing gimmick.

### Is InterServer good for WordPress?

Yes. WordPress installs in a single click from cPanel, SSL is included and auto-renewed, and the server-level Cloudflare integration plus caching keep WordPress sites loading well. The Standard plan comfortably handles a typical WordPress blog, and because the same infrastructure runs across all plans, the budget tier is not deliberately slowed down.
