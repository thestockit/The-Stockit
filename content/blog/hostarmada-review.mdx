## Cloud infrastructure and data centres

HostArmada operates its own cloud platform, and it has spread that platform across a genuinely global set of locations. At the time of writing, HostArmada lists data centres spanning North America (including the US East Coast and West Coast, plus Canada), Europe (London, Frankfurt and several other locations), Asia (Singapore, Mumbai) and Australia (Sydney). That choice is more valuable than most people realise. We have reviewed hosts that are technically great but run all their servers out of one or two US cities, which punishes every visitor in Europe or Asia. HostArmada lets you pick the region closest to your audience at signup, which is the single most effective thing any host can do for speed — no CDN can fully compensate for a server on the other side of the planet.

There are two honest caveats. First, not every location runs every plan — some of the more exotic regions are reserved for higher-tier plans or cloud VPS customers, so check availability before you sign up for the cheapest tier. Second, HostArmada does not publish its data-centre naming the way SiteGround does with Google Cloud; you are getting HostArmada's own infrastructure, which is fine, but you are also trusting a smaller independent company with a shorter track record than the giants. So far the record is good, but it is a bet you should make with your eyes open.

<Callout type="info" title="Why TTFB matters">
TTFB is the time between the browser sending a request and the server starting to respond. It is the largest single contributor to Google's Core Web Vitals LCP score on most sites, and a 200 ms improvement in TTFB is often worth more than any theme optimisation you can buy. Choosing a data centre near your audience is one of the cheapest speed upgrades available — which is why HostArmada's multi-region footprint matters.
</Callout>

## Security: malware scans and WAF included

HostArmada bundles a genuine security stack into every shared plan instead of selling it as an upsell. The headline feature is the company's own **WebShield** security suite, which includes:

- **A web application firewall (WAF)** filtering malicious requests before they reach your site
- **Free malware scanning**, run on demand or on a schedule, plus one-click malware cleanup if something is found
- **Intrusion detection and DDoS protection** at the network level
- **Free SSL certificates** with auto-renewal on every plan

On top of that, **free daily backups** act as your safety net: if anything goes wrong — a bad plugin update, a hacked file, a mistake in the file manager — you restore yesterday's snapshot from the control panel in a couple of minutes. For a shared host, that level of included protection is well above the bare minimum we see on many budget platforms.

As with any host, platform-level security is only half the story. A strong WAF and malware scanner will not protect you from your own weak plugin passwords or an abandoned plugin with a known vulnerability. Pair HostArmada's built-in protection with sensible WordPress hygiene — keep everything updated, use a strong admin password and limit logins — and you are in good shape.
