Businesses in Pakistan have different payment requirements depending on what they sell, where their customers are and how they want to collect payments. An ecommerce store, service business, marketplace and custom website may all need different payment methods and integration options.

A payment gateway connects a business website or app with the systems used to process digital payments. Depending on the provider, customers may be able to pay through cards, mobile wallets, bank payments, payment links or Raast.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
There is no single payment gateway that fits every Pakistani business. Before choosing one, compare payment methods, website compatibility, integration options, onboarding requirements, pricing and settlement terms.
</Callout>

## How to Choose a Payment Gateway in Pakistan

Start with what your customers need rather than choosing a provider based only on brand recognition.

### Payment methods

Check whether you need cards, Easypaisa, JazzCash, bank payments, Raast or a combination of these.

### Website platform

Your integration options depend on whether you use WooCommerce, Shopify, a custom website or another platform.

### Hosted checkout or API

A hosted payment page can simplify implementation. An API gives developers more control over the payment flow but requires more technical work.

### Onboarding

Ask what business documents, identity checks, website information and settlement details are required before applying.

### Total cost

Compare the full commercial arrangement rather than only one transaction percentage. Ask about taxes, refunds, chargebacks, setup costs and settlement arrangements.

### Reporting and support

Make sure you can track transactions, reconcile payments and investigate failed or reversed transactions.

## Payment Gateway for Ecommerce, WooCommerce and Shopify

For an ecommerce business, the gateway needs to work reliably from checkout through payment confirmation, refunds and order updates.

### WooCommerce

WooCommerce supports payment integrations through plugins and APIs. PayFast currently documents WooCommerce integration, while Easypaisa also documents WordPress and WooCommerce integration options.

Before installing a plugin, check that it is maintained for your current WooCommerce and PHP versions and that completed payments are correctly reflected in the order system.

### Shopify

Shopify payment integrations depend on the providers and apps available to merchants in Pakistan. PayFast currently documents Shopify integration on its checkout offering.

Before choosing an integration, confirm the supported payment methods, maintenance status and any additional costs.

## How Payment Gateway Integration Works

The exact process depends on the provider, but most implementations follow a similar path:

1. Choose the provider and required payment methods.
2. Register and complete onboarding/KYC.
3. Receive merchant credentials or integration access.
4. Configure the hosted checkout, plugin or API.
5. Test successful, failed and cancelled transactions.
6. Test refunds and payment-status callbacks.
7. Run a live test transaction.
8. Start accepting customer payments.

For custom API integrations, your backend should verify the payment result before marking an order as paid.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a payment gateway in Pakistan?

A payment gateway is a service that helps a business accept digital payments through a website or app. Depending on the provider, this can include cards, mobile wallets, bank payments and Raast.

### Which payment gateway is best for a business in Pakistan?

There is no universal answer. Compare payment methods, website compatibility, integration requirements, onboarding and commercial terms before choosing.

### What is the best online payment gateway in Pakistan?

The appropriate option depends on the business and its customers. A-Pay, PayFast, Easypaisa and JazzCash all document payment solutions for businesses in Pakistan, but their products and integration models differ.

### Can I integrate a payment gateway with WooCommerce?

Yes. PayFast and Easypaisa currently document WooCommerce integration options. Other providers may be connected through APIs or other integration methods depending on the provider and your setup.

### Can I integrate a payment gateway with Shopify?

Yes, but the available integration depends on the provider and its current Shopify support. PayFast currently documents Shopify integration.

### Can businesses accept JazzCash and Easypaisa online?

Yes. JazzCash documents an online Payment Gateway, while Easypaisa documents Online Payment Gateway and merchant payment solutions.

### Which payment gateways support Raast?

A-Pay, PayFast and JazzCash currently document Raast-related payment options. Easypaisa also provides QR payment functionality through its merchant platform. Exact availability depends on the product and merchant setup.

### How much does a payment gateway cost in Pakistan?

Costs vary by provider, payment method and merchant agreement. Some providers publish rates while others require merchants to confirm their applicable pricing. Always request the complete fee schedule before signing up.

### What documents do I need for a payment gateway?

Requirements vary by provider, but businesses should expect some combination of business information, identity verification, website details and settlement-account information.
